merged dispatch table. No new hot spot appeared from the fork-chain redesign itself (the top leaf,
`Matcher.attemptMatch`/`find`'s own overhead, is unrelated to this change), and the old
`DispatchMatcherConstruct`/`MultiDispatchingMatcherConstruct.getNext` leaves are simply gone,
replaced by ordinary `matchNext`/fork `match()` calls. `llkMatch` itself improved slightly this
round (0.044 -> 0.037 ms/op) -- consistent with the advisor's flagged risk (a fork chain trading one
binary search over k targets for up to k-1 sequential membership tests) not materializing as a
regression on this corpus, at least at its actual candidate-count distribution.
