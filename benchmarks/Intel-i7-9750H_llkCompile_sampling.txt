Read: the old top leaves (`buildLoopMatcher`'s own `forEachRange` lambdas, building the
now-removed `DispatchMatcherConstruct`/loop dispatch table) are gone entirely -- `buildForkChain`
builds its chain by walking the candidate list directly, with no `forEachRange` pass of its own.
The new top leaf is `buildLoopEntryMap`'s own `forEachRange` lambda (projecting the merged
entry-point map down to plain `Boolean` values for `entryMap` -- unrelated to this change, still
needed for ambiguity detection), followed by `ArrayCodePointMap` construction inside
`CodePointMapBuilder.build` (both the parser's own character-range building and
`mergeEntryPoints`'s ambiguity-detection merge) and parser overhead. `gc.alloc.rate.norm` dropped
from 1,886,560 B/op (previous baseline, see git history) to 1,337,120 B/op -- consistent with no
longer allocating a `CodePointMap<MatcherConstruct>` dispatch table (and its backing
`ArrayCodePointMap`) for every union/loop-entry node. `llkCompile` itself improved from
0.646 ms/op to 0.480 ms/op this round.
